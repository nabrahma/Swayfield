# Swayfield

**One-click animated grass for Blender, with wind you can art-direct.**

Select a mesh, click **Add Grass**, press play. Swayfield grows long, soft grass with a strong crosswind, bright gust streaks racing across the field and crests that glow where the grass bends flat.

## What you get

- **One-click grass** on any mesh, fully non-destructive.
- **Eight wind types**, led by Rolling Meadow.
- **Effectors**: Vortex, Radial Blast, Trample and Shelter.
- **Painting and masks**: density, length, wind strength and colour variation, plus slope, altitude, noise, image, proximity and camera-distance masks.
- **Three material modes**: Procedural, Image Maps and Single Color.
- **Optimised Mode** to keep the viewport interactive on big fields.

!!! tip "Renders without the add-on"
    Everything lives in Geometry Nodes groups and materials saved inside your .blend file. Open it on a machine or render farm without Swayfield and it still renders. You only need the add-on to edit with the Swayfield panel.
