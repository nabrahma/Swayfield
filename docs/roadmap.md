# Roadmap

Swayfield 0.1.0 has all the must-have features from the product plan and most of the should-have ones. These are not in 0.1.0 yet. Nothing here has a date.

## Planned

- **Texture-paint masks**: paint an image instead of vertex weights, for low-poly surfaces where vertices are too coarse.
- **Edge offset**: no grass within a set distance of open mesh borders.
- **Apply and Alembic export**: convert the grass to real curves or mesh, and bake the wind to Alembic for other software or render farms.
- **Viewport-follow culling** (experimental): cull to the 3D view instead of the scene camera.
- **Season slider and dry-grass presets**: a quick green-to-yellow tint.
- **Flower and weed layers**: a small biome system that mixes other plants into the grass.

## Smaller items

- **Weighted instance picking**: choose how often each mesh in a Custom Instances collection is used.
- **Preset buttons**: panel buttons to save, apply and delete presets.

- **Pause wind while navigating**: freeze wind while you orbit or paint.
- **Trample recovery time**: let grass stand back up slowly after a Trample effector moves away.

## Ideas, not promises

Want something else? Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/nabrahma/Swayfield/issues) and say what shot you are trying to make.
